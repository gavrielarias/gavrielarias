Specialized support for your brief

When an agency or in-house team has a defined project and needs additional production expertise, I can join to handle an agreed part of the work. I provide motion graphics, video editing, design and digital production support, with scope and responsibilities set around your brief.

Motion graphics for agencies

Motion can support a campaign video, branded content or a digital experience. Share your creative concept, brand guidelines, available assets and intended formats. We agree on what needs to be animated, how it will be reviewed and which final files the project requires before production begins.

Video, design and digital production

The contribution can extend beyond motion to video editing, brand assets, websites and other digital deliverables. You can bring an established approach or a specific creative and technical challenge. We define the part I will own and how it connects with the work your team is already producing.

A workflow agreed with your team
