Details

Project-based or longer-term?

Both. Hire me for a specific deliverable or as a freelance contractor over a longer engagement. We agree on availability, responsibilities, deadlines and the review process.

What should I send?

Your creative concept, brand guidelines, available assets, intended formats and the deadline. That’s enough to confirm availability and fit.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I work within the agency’s brief and review process, under its brand and with an NDA when needed.
