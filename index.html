Frequently asked
questions

What kind of projects do you take on?

I work on complete campaigns, brand content, video, motion, websites, and digital experiences. I also handle specific production needs for agencies and creative teams.

Can you handle an entire project?

Yes. I can shape the approach and build the agreed deliverables for a launch, campaign or digital experience. We define the scope and responsibilities before production starts.

Can I hire you for a specific task?

Yes. I can join a defined project for focused production support across brand, video, motion or web. We agree on the deliverables, workflow and schedule together.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I’m based in Costa Rica and work with brands and agencies internationally. We agree on communication and review times at the start.
