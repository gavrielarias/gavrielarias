Details

Is this a good fit for my brand?

It suits brands that need one partner to take responsibility for a complete campaign, launch or digital experience. If you already have a defined concept and need one specific piece, I can support that brief too.

Do I need every format?

No. A launch does not have to include every format. We choose the deliverables that serve your goal and where the work will be used.

How does payment work?

25% upfront to reserve the production dates, the rest according to the milestones in your proposal. Once paid, the final deliverables and their usage rights are yours.

What if the direction isn’t right?

You approve the direction before full production. If it still doesn’t work for you after the review rounds, you can stop there: the deposit covers the work done and you owe nothing more.

Do you work with international brands?

Yes. I work from Costa Rica (GMT-6, same as US Central time) with brands in the US, Canada and Europe, in English or Spanish.
